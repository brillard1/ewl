\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Fine-tuning a pre-trained transformer with LoRA~\cite{hu2022lora} is not
training from scratch: the model arrives with strong priors, and sample
informativeness shifts during training.
Some examples become \emph{mastered} (loss $\to 0$); others are
\emph{conflicting} (labels irreconcilable with the prior, e.g.\ noise); a
shifting minority sits at the \emph{adaptation frontier}, where loss is
actively declining.
Standard SFT treats all three equally, diluting each mini-batch gradient.
Prior approaches fall short: curriculum/self-paced
learning~\cite{bengio2009curriculum,kumar2010self} weights by loss
\emph{magnitude}, conflating frontier with noise; meta-reweighting~\cite{ren2018learning,shu2019meta}
needs a clean held-out set and doubles compute; Dataset
Cartography~\cite{swayamdipta2020dataset} requires a preliminary pass.

We propose \textbf{EWL (Example Weighting via Learning Progress)}, an online
method that tracks per-sample loss velocity via an EMA and gates it with a
\emph{LoRA gradient proxy} --- the mean Frobenius norm of adapter gradients,
acting as an adaptive temperature that sharpens weights during rapid
adaptation and decays to uniform near convergence.
This gating is essential: without it, aggressive early reweighting
destabilises training.
EWL requires no meta-gradients, no held-out data, and no inference cost.

Experiments on three fine-grained benchmarks with ViT-Small/16~\cite{dosovitskiy2021vit} yield two
findings.
First, the gradient proxy is not optional: ablating it causes a $-6.76\%$
drop on FGVC-Aircraft~\cite{maji2013aircraft}.
Second, the magnitude of EWL's noise-robustness gain is
\emph{dataset-conditional}: on Aircraft, where inter-class similarity
sustains a wide frontier, EWL improves over SFT by $+4.3$--$6.8\%$ under
10--40\% label noise; on CUB-200 \cite{wah2011cub} the advantage is smaller ($+1.9$--$2.8\%$)
and realised earlier in training; on Stanford Dogs \cite{khosla2011dogs} EWL merely matches SFT
within noise.
On clean data EWL matches SFT within std at $5.9\times$ step-time overhead
and zero additional GPU memory.

\noindent\textbf{Contributions:} (i)~\textbf{EWL}, an online LoRA
sample-weighting method based on loss velocity gated by a LoRA gradient
proxy; (ii)~evidence that the proxy is \textbf{essential for stability};
(iii)~\textbf{dataset-conditional noise-robustness gains} tied to
adaptation frontier width, with practical usage guidance; (iv)~full ablations over
rank, temperature, EMA decay, and class imbalance (Appendix).
