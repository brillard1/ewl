\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Progress signal.}
Let $\ell_i^{(t)}$ be the per-sample cross-entropy loss at step $t$.
EWL maintains a per-sample EMA as a smoothed loss history, updated
\emph{before} computing the signal:
\begin{align}
  \mu_i^{(t)} &= \alpha\,\mu_i^{(t-1)} + (1{-}\alpha)\,\ell_i^{(t)},
  \label{eq:ema} \\
  s_i^{\text{rel}} &=
    \frac{\mu_i^{(t-1)} - \ell_i^{(t)}}{|\mu_i^{(t-1)}| + \varepsilon}
    \cdot\,\mu_i^{(t)}, \quad \varepsilon = 10^{-8}.
  \label{eq:signal}
\end{align}
Dividing by $|\mu_i^{(t-1)}|$ makes the signal scale-invariant.
The three sample types map cleanly onto $s_i^{\text{rel}}$:
\emph{mastered} ($\mu\!\approx\!\ell\!\approx\!0 \Rightarrow s \approx 0$),
\emph{conflicting} ($\ell\!\approx\!\mu\!\gg\!0 \Rightarrow s \approx 0$ or
negative --- suppressed without an explicit noise model), and \emph{frontier}
($\ell < \mu$ consistently $\Rightarrow s > 0$, upweighted).

\paragraph{LoRA gradient proxy.}
The progress signal alone is not a safe weighting scheme: before the EMA has
accumulated history, aggressive reweighting amplifies unstable early gradients.
We multiply $s_i^{\text{rel}}$ by a LoRA gradient proxy computed from the
\emph{previous} backward pass (avoiding a circular dependency):
\begin{equation}
  g^{(t)} = \frac{1}{|\Lambda|}\sum_{\theta \in \Lambda} \|\nabla_\theta\|_F,
  \quad s_i = s_i^{\text{rel}} \cdot g^{(t-1)},
  \label{eq:proxy}
\end{equation}
where $\Lambda$ is the set of LoRA parameter tensors ($A,B$) across all
targeted layers.
Because $z$-normalisation before the softmax would cancel a constant factor
--- $(gs - \overline{gs})/\sigma_{gs} = (s - \bar s)/\sigma_s$ --- the proxy
functions as an \emph{adaptive temperature}:
$1/\tau_{\text{eff}} = g^{(t-1)}/\tau$.
Weights sharpen toward the frontier during rapid adaptation and flatten toward
uniform as $g \to 0$, gracefully recovering the SFT baseline.
Ablating $g$ causes a $-6.76\%$ drop on Aircraft
(\S\ref{sec:proxy_ablation}), empirically confirming this gating is essential.

\paragraph{Weighted loss.}
The combined signal is converted to per-sample weights by softmax and applied
as a batch-size-rescaled weighted loss:
\begin{equation}
  w_i = \frac{\exp(s_i / \tau)}{\sum_j \exp(s_j / \tau)}, \quad
  \mathcal{L}_{\text{EWL}} = B \sum_i w_i\,\ell_i.
  \label{eq:loss}
\end{equation}
A $K$-step warmup keeps $w_i{=}1/B$ while the EMA accumulates history.
Full pseudocode and a theoretical grounding (gradient-variance and LoRA
subspace alignment arguments) are deferred to Appendix~\ref{app:method}.
